# What to the Slave Is the Fourth of July?

An invitation for July 4, 2026, built around Frederick Douglass's speech of July 5, 1852, in Corinthian Hall, Rochester. Invited to speak on the Fourth, he chose the Fifth.

**Visit:** [douglass.vercel.app](https://douglass.vercel.app)

## What it is

- **The speech, set against the date.** The page counts the years from his speech to the Semiquincentennial: 174 years on July 4, 2026.
- **The archive behind it.** 44 archival images from the Library of Congress, the Metropolitan Museum of Art, NYPL, the Smithsonian, the National Archives and the Art Institute of Chicago. Douglass was the most photographed American of the 19th century (Stauffer, Trodd and Bernier, *Picturing Frederick Douglass*, 2015).
- **Built in the open:** a single static page with its fonts and images, no framework.

**Radical Imagination**, a cultural technology studio in Queens. [radicalimagination.xyz](https://radicalimagination.xyz)
