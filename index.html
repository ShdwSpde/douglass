What to the Slave Is the Fourth of July?

Delivered on July 5, 1852. Staged in VR on July 4, 2026 — at the exact moment the United States turns 250.

Douglass was invited to speak on July 4. He chose July 5. The refusal was the point.

174 years later, an immersive experience stages the speech as it was heard: the pageantry of Corinthian Hall, the rhetorical turn, the coffle passing by the celebration, the Constitution in flames, the Jubilee dawn. Seven scenes, built in WebXR, voiced with ElevenLabs. The speech is the spine. The room is the argument.

Launching at the 250th anniversary. Free. No login, no download, no app store. Enter from your browser.
